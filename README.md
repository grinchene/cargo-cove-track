# CargoCove

CargoCove is a decentralized supply chain verification platform that revolutionizes transparency and compliance monitoring across global trade networks. The platform combines blockchain technology with AI-powered risk assessment and real-time IoT sensor integration to create dynamic compliance scores that automatically adjust based on real-world conditions, supplier performance history, and regulatory changes across multiple jurisdictions.

The platform implements a sophisticated three-tier verification system powered by smart contracts and immutable blockchain records. Tier 1 utilizes automated IoT sensors for environmental monitoring and location tracking, Tier 2 employs computer vision and machine learning to analyze facility compliance through uploaded imagery, and Tier 3 integrates third-party audit data and certifications. The predictive compliance engine identifies potential ethical violations before they occur by analyzing patterns in supplier behavior, environmental data, and market conditions.

CargoCove enables brands to offer consumers complete product journey transparency through QR code access, including carbon footprint data, labor conditions, and origin verification. The platform generates immutable compliance reports that satisfy regulatory requirements across different markets while providing actionable insights for supply chain optimization. Key stakeholders including brands, suppliers, NGOs, and consumers can participate in governance through a weighted voting mechanism that sets and updates ethical standards collaboratively.
